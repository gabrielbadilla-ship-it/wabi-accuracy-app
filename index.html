1. Validation 35 pts
✓ Pass
Customer sentiment
Lead approved a standard resolution without considering the customer's emotional state or escalation level — strong frustration, churn risk, or social media threat.
Prior escalations
Lead approved without checking whether the customer had previous escalations for the same issue, which could indicate a recurring problem.
Customer profile
Lead approved without verifying the customer's profile — ARPU, LTR, order count, or plan type — which determines the appropriate compensation level.
Account & Platform Records
Lead approved without checking internal tools (Braze, Mixpanel, Admin) when account-level information was relevant to the case.
Delivery Evidence
Lead approved without verifying delivery evidence — Onfleet tracking, POD, carrier side conversation, or Logistics coordination.
Customer Evidence
Lead approved without asking for supporting documentation from the customer — photos of damaged meals, broken seals, foreign objects, or screenshots.
2. Issue Resolution 20 pts
✓ Pass
Compensation type
Lead approved the wrong form of compensation — e.g. credit when a refund was required, or a discount when a credit was the correct resolution.
Compensation amount
Lead approved an amount that doesn't match the case — too high (overcompensation) or too low (undercompensation) given the customer's profile and the issue.
Compensation omission
No compensation was approved at all when the case clearly warranted one — either by rejecting the request or responding without indicating any compensation. If a compensation was approved but an extra request (discount, promo, retention) went unanswered, use Incomplete Lead response.
3. Process & Escalation 35 pts
✓ Pass
Sup/Management escalation
Lead approved a resolution that required supervisor or manager approval — custom amount, policy exception, or high-value compensation — without flagging it.
Incorrect Process
Lead applied a process that doesn't correspond to this case, or applied a standard process when a temporary override or incident guideline was in effect.
⚠️ Before selecting: if the process is unclear or undocumented, do not mark this as Fail. Flag the gap in #cx-training-process-update instead. If selecting, please include the link to the correct process in your feedback.
Missing next steps
Lead approved the compensation but did not indicate a required escalation (FSQA, Logistics, Jira) or a needed account update to fully resolve the case.
Escalation reason
Lead resolved the case without ensuring the escalation reason was correct — approved an incorrect reason, left it unspecified, or indicated an outdated classification.
4. Coaching & Guidance 10 pts
✓ Pass
Validation source
Lead validated missing information independently but did not instruct the Specialist on where to find it or how to include it in future Wabis.
Incomplete Lead response
The Specialist asked a question or made a request beyond the main compensation (e.g. discount, promo restoration, retention offer, process question) and the Lead didn't answer it.
Specialist knowledge gap
Lead's response revealed a gap in the Specialist's process or policy knowledge and the Lead did not take the opportunity to clarify or educate.

Optional when the review has no fails — e.g. good practices worth recognizing or a tip for the Lead.

    What is Wabi Accuracy?

    We review how Leads resolve Wabis — and use it to coach.

    A Wabi is an escalation where a CX Specialist asks a Lead to approve, deny or give guidance on a compensation. Every week, supervisors review a sample of Wabis with a shared rubric to check that the Lead's decision was validated, correctly resolved, followed the right process, and coached the Specialist.

    We evaluate the Lead's resolution, not the Specialist's work. A Specialist mistake only counts when the Lead should have caught or corrected it.

    Rules

    • Each category is Pass or Fail: full points with no root cause marked, 0 with at least one.
    • The total is the sum of the four categories. Target: 90.
    • Mark a fail only when there is a real error in the resolution.
    • Feedback & Coaching Notes are required when a category fails, optional on a 100.
    • Any fail generates feedback for the Lead's supervisor.
    • Calibration reviews are scored the same way but never generate feedback.

    Quick decision guide

    Writing the feedback comment

    1. What was incorrect or incomplete — the specific action or omission.
    2. Why it matters — impact on the customer, the Specialist or reporting.
    3. What good looks like — the correct resolution for next time.

    Avoid conditional reasoning ("if this was…"), unverified assumptions, and repeating the rubric definition instead of describing the case.

    Pending definitions

    • Escalation reason level: whether the ER must be specified up to L3 or L4. Until it's defined, a correct ER up to L3 is not a fail.
